FAQ

Where do you currently deliver and how long does it take?
We currently offer local delivery exclusively around the SCMS School of Engineering and Technology, Karukutty campus. You can expect your order to arrive within 3 to 4 days!
What is the difference between a Bar and a Bite?
Our Bars are full-sized portions perfect for a meal replacement or serious post-workout recovery. Our Bites are smaller, half-sized portions—great for a quick snack between classes or a light energy boost.
Which bar is best for Protein or Low Sugar?
If you are looking for maximum protein intake, go for the Dark Rush bar! It is specially formulated to give you the highest protein content for recovery. If you are watching your sugar intake, our Seed Rush flavor has the lowest sugar of the entire lineup.
